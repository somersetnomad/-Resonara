Operator’s guide · R-1

Your voice, measured. Your tone, tuned.

Resonara is a two-part sound instrument that runs in your browser. The input bay listens to your voice and finds its fundamental frequency, the steady base pitch your voice settles on, along with how steady it is and how rich its overtones are. The output bay then plays pure, carefully tuned tones and binaural beats, built from presets or from your own voice, for relaxation, focus and meditation.

How to use it
  1. Put on headphones

    Binaural beats only work when each ear hears its own tone. Start with the level knob low and raise it to a comfortable volume.

  2. Press Listen and hold a vowel

    Sing a relaxed “Ah” or “Oh” at a natural pitch for 3 to 5 seconds. Allow microphone access when your browser asks. No mic? Use Load file with a voice recording.

  3. Read your results

    The large number is your fundamental (F₀) in hertz. The needle shows how close you are to the nearest musical note, the pitch line shows how steady you held it, and the harmonic bars show the richness of your tone.

  4. Tune the output

    Press Tune to voice to set the carrier to your own fundamental, or pick one of the 12 presets. Turn the knobs to adjust: drag, scroll, or click a value to type it. Hold ⇧ for fine control.

  5. Play and settle in

    Choose a timer, press Play (or Space), close your eyes and breathe. The tone fades out gently when the session ends.

What you gain
Know your fundamental

Discover the exact frequency and note your voice naturally rests on, measured to a tenth of a hertz.

See how steady you are

The stability reading and pitch line show how calm and controlled your voice is. Watch them change as you relax.

Tones tuned to you

Build a session around your own voice’s frequency, rather than a generic tone made for everyone.

Guided brainwave sessions

Twelve presets span delta, theta, alpha, beta and gamma beats for rest, meditation, calm focus and alert work.

A simple daily ritual

Set a timer, put the screen down and let the tone fade out on its own: an easy 5 to 30 minute reset.

Private by design

Everything runs on your device. Your voice is never recorded, stored or uploaded, and there’s no account to make.

Headphones

Needed for binaural beats. Pure tones with no beat (the Solfeggio presets) play fine on speakers.

Microphone

Works on a secure page (https) in its own browser tab. If it’s blocked, the instrument tells you why and how to fix it.

Best results

Use a quiet room, sit comfortably, and keep the volume low. Short daily sessions work better than occasional long ones.

Please note

Resonara is for relaxation and exploration, not medical care. If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, check with your doctor before using beats.
